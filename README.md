📊 Sales Performance & Revenue Growth Dashboard (Power BI)

🚀 Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze revenue, profit, sales trends, and business performance.

---

🎯 Business Objectives

- Track overall revenue and profit performance
- Compare actual sales vs target sales
- Identify top-performing categories and channels
- Monitor monthly sales trends
- Evaluate regional and executive performance

---

📌 Key Metrics (KPIs)

- 💰 Total Revenue: ₹23.9M
- 📈 Total Profit: ₹5.98M
- 📦 Total Units Sold: 931
- 🎯 Achievement: 32.73%
- 📊 Profit Margin: 9%

---

📊 Dashboard Features

🔝 KPI Cards

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Units
- Achievement %
- Profit Margin %

📉 Visualizations

- Bar Chart → Profit by Category
- Donut Chart → Revenue by Channel
- Column Chart → Actual vs Target Sales
- Line Chart → Monthly Revenue Trend
- Bar Chart → Sales by Executive

🎛 Filters (Slicers)

- Region
- Category
- Channel
- Month

---

🔍 Key Insights

- Clothing category generates highest profit
- Online channel contributes ~60% revenue
- Achievement below 100% indicates missed targets
- Revenue trend shows decline after February

---

🛠 Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Excel (Data Source)
